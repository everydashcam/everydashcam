Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-01

1. About the developer

everydashcam is a web app for viewing dashcam recordings, operated by an individual developer ("the developer"). For any data-protection question or to exercise the rights described below, contact privacy@everydashcam.app. A Data Protection Officer has not been appointed (GDPR Art. 37 criteria do not apply).

2. Data collection

Your video files and GPS logs never leave your device. The app runs entirely in your browser.

Only anonymous, aggregate usage data about the app itself is collected:

  • Cloudflare Web Analytics - page-view counts, referrers, browser/OS aggregates at the network edge. Cookieless. This integration is deployment-specific and runs only where it is enabled. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Crash reports (Sentry) - when an error occurs, the app sends an anonymized diagnostic report to help diagnose and fix it: the error type, an anonymized stack trace, and your browser/OS. No video, no GPS, no file names, no coordinates. Processed by Sentry on EU servers (Germany); kept about 30 days. Legal basis: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). On by default; you can opt out any time via Settings.

The developer does not collect video content, GPS coordinates, file names, accounts, payment data, or sensitive categories (GDPR Art. 9). Personal data is not sold, remarketing audiences are not built, and ad personalization signals are not sent to advertising platforms. Providing data is not required - the app works either way. The service is not directed at children.

When you contact the developer or send a report. Separately from the automatic analytics above: if you email the developer, or use the in-app "report a problem" or "help add my dashcam" buttons, the developer receives what you choose to send - your message and any file you attach, which may include a list of the file and folder names on your memory card, their sizes and dates. This is always started by you, you see it before it is sent, and it never includes your video or GPS coordinates. This information is used only to reply to you and to add support for your dashcam.

3. Cookies

everydashcam sets no cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics and crash reporting are both cookieless, and your preferences (language, settings) are stored only in your browser's local storage on your own device - never sent to the developer.

4. Retention and international transfers

Retention: Cloudflare Web Analytics aggregates per provider terms; crash reports about 30 days (see above).

Transfers: Cloudflare Web Analytics data is anonymous and aggregated at the network edge; crash reports are processed by Sentry on EU servers (Germany). Neither requires additional transfer safeguards.

5. Your rights

You have the rights granted by GDPR Art. 15-21 (and equivalent UK GDPR rights), and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority (list at EDPB). California residents have rights under CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (personal information is not sold or shared).

To exercise:

  • Turn off crash reports - Settings (gear icon) → switch off "Crash reports".
  • Anything else - email privacy@everydashcam.app (response within one month, Art. 12(3)).

Users are not profiled and no automated decisions are made about them (Art. 22).

6. Third-party services

Maps come from OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap or, where available, Yandex Maps. You can change the provider in Settings. If a provider is unavailable, the app may switch to another automatically. The active map provider receives your IP address, the site address and the visible map area.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service or applicable laws change. The effective date at the top will be updated.
